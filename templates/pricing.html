How to get access

  1. Create your account

    Register and confirm your email. You will receive an account ID.

  2. Message the admin

    Open Telegram from this page. Confirm the price, access period and payment details in the chat.

  3. Pay, then start practising

    After the admin confirms payment, they activate your access. Sign in to the same account and open your dashboard.

A few useful details

How much does full access cost?

The admin confirms the current price and available access periods in Telegram before payment.

When will my access open?

Activation is manual. The admin confirms it in the chat after checking your payment. Opening Telegram alone does not activate access.

Will access renew automatically?

No. For this manual payment option, contact the admin again when you want to extend access. There is no automatic charge.

I paid, but the tests are still locked. What should I do?

Check that you are signed in to the account whose ID you sent. If the admin has confirmed activation and the tests are still locked, message them with that ID.

Can I use my phone and computer?

Yes. Sign in to the same account to use your access and saved progress on both.
