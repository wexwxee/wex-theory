All questions and answers

Do I need an account to try it?

No. The {{ f.free_questions }}-question starter test, all {{ f.lessons }} sign lessons and the ten-word demo open without signing up. A free account is separate from paid access to the full library.

Can I practise in Russian?

Questions, statements and explanations have a Russian helper next to the English. Which language you can sit the official test in is a separate matter — read the exam language guide before you book.

Is this the official theory test?

No. WEXTheory is independent practice material for category B. Use it alongside your driving school and official guidance; a score here does not predict or guarantee an official result.

How do I get full access?

Create an account, then message the admin on Telegram from the access page. You agree on the price and period first; the admin activates your account after confirming payment.
