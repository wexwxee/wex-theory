Before you book

Which language can you take the theory test in?

Choose from the supported languages and confirm the format with your driving school. WEXTheory offers English-first practice with a Russian helper translation; Russian is not an official test language.

Five languages

Danish, English, Faroese, Greenlandic and German. No other language is offered for the test.

Interpreter exceptions

Digital theory tests do not use interpreters. Faroese and Greenlandic tests use an interpreter in the analog format; sign-language arrangements are also available through the authority.

Digital test: three

Danish and English are offered at the digital test locations. Digital theory tests in German are held in Aabenraa. Audio is available.

What the rule says

The language rules changed on 1 January 2025. An interpreter does not make an unsupported language available: for example, an earlier test with a Persian interpreter does not allow a new test in Persian. Ask your school to book the correct language and any permitted special arrangements.

  • Theory and practical tests are held in Danish, English, Faroese, Greenlandic or German.
  • Digital theory tests do not use interpreters. Faroese, Greenlandic and sign-language arrangements must be booked through the authority.
  • For the digital theory test, German is available in Aabenraa; Danish and English are offered across the digital locations.

If your first language is Russian, Ukrainian, Arabic, Polish…

Choose a supported language you understand well. If you choose English, practise reading and listening to exam phrases as well as studying the traffic rules. Use the Russian helper to resolve unclear wording, then try the English question again without it.

  • Practise the questions in English from the start, not translated in your head.
  • Learn the recurring exam terms as vocabulary, separately from the rules.
  • Use audio, because the digital test can read the question to you.

How WEXTheory is built for this

The library is English-first, in the wording style you meet at the test, with a Russian helper translation you can open when a sentence blocks you rather than as the default. Exam Words keeps the vocabulary in Danish, English and Russian side by side, with cards, quiz and audio.
