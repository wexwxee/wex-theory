Test day

Cost, booking, and the two test formats

Same rules either way: 25 questions, 25 minutes, five mistakes allowed. What differs is whether you sit in front of paper or a touchscreen — and that changes how the questions feel.

Start free test Which language you sit it in What changed in 2026
1,600 kr state fee

Færdselsstyrelsen lists 1,600 kr for the category B driving test. Driving-school fees come on top — ask your school what its price includes.

Booked by your school

Tests are booked through the official booking system, normally by your driving school, not by you directly.

25 min, max 5 mistakes

Both formats: 25 questions in 25 minutes, and you need at least 20 correct to pass.

The two formats side by side

WEXTheory provides its own study questions and explanations. Try the authority's official demo as well to become familiar with its test interface.

Where the digital test runs

Færdselsstyrelsen currently lists sixteen digital locations. Ask your school for the digital format when booking. German digital theory tests are held in Aabenraa; Danish and English are offered across the digital locations.

Before you go

  • Bring valid photo ID, your approved P23 application and the signed module or lesson plan. For a digital test, bring MitID and check that you can log in. Confirm any exemption or special requirements with your school using the authority's current checklist.
  • Færdselsstyrelsen offers an official category B practice test with a guide and 25 question scenes. Use it to try the navigation before test day: dtp.fstyr.dk.
  • Confirm your appointment time and arrive early enough for the document and identity checks.

Practise the rhythm, not just the rules

Twenty-five questions in twenty-five minutes is roughly a minute each. The mixed Exam Mode in the full library runs at that pace.
